### 3. QUALITY ASSURANCE (Mental Sandbox)
Before outputting, verify the following constraints:

* **Constraint 1: Uniqueness (Anti-Redundancy)**
    * *Anti-Reciprocity:* Do NOT output [('A', 'Impacts', 'B'), ('B', 'Is Impacted By', 'A')]. Output ONLY the active direction.
    * *Anti-Synonym:* Do NOT output multiple triplets for the same fact using different verbs.

* **Constraint 2: Semantic Validity**
    * *Subject-Verb Logic:* Ensure the Entity Type allows the Action.
    * *Bad:* [('Stock Price', 'Announces', 'Earnings')] -> A price cannot announce.
    * *Fix:* [('Company', 'Announces', 'Earnings'), ('Earnings', 'Impacts', 'Stock Price')].

* **Constraint 3: Atomic Granularity**
    * *Bad:* [('Fed', 'Raises Rates and Causes Crash', 'Market')] -> Too complex.
    * *Fix:* [('Fed', 'Raises', 'Interest Rates'), ('Interest Rates', 'Negative Impact On', 'Market')].

* **Constraint 4: String Hygiene**
    * **NO** special characters (like @, #, $, %, &).
    * **SPACES ARE REQUIRED** between words.
    * **NO** apostrophes. (e.g., 'Lowes' not 'Lowe's').
    * *Bad:* 'AdvancedMicroDevices', 'CompetesWith'
    * *Fix:* 'Advanced Micro Devices', 'Competes With'
